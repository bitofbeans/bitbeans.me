Notes and Information:

▬ Controls + Notes ▬
• Menu ▬ Point and Click • Movement ▬ WASD or Arrow keys • Reset Player ▬ HOLD R • Teleport ▬ While touching a purple pad, click and drag from your player to where you want to teleport.
▬ Editor Keybinds + Notes ▬
• Platforms ▬ 1-8 • Checkpoint ▬ Z • Hand Tool ▬ X • Delete Tool ▬ C • Play/Edit ▬ E • Settings ▬ Q Level autosaves every 30s
